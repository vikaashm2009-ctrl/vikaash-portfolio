01

The Beginning

Vikaash chose Computer Science for one reason — he wanted to know how an idea turns into something real. A thought becomes a line of code, a line of code becomes a system, and a system becomes something people actually use. That gap between imagination and execution is what pulled him in.

He's still figuring out exactly where he wants to specialise — and he's comfortable saying that out loud. Right now, the plan isn't to have all the answers. It's to explore widely, build small things often, and let curiosity point the direction.

No major shipped products. No hackathon trophies yet. What he does have is a habit of pulling things apart to see how they work, and the patience to sit with a problem until it clicks.

Current status Currently exploring. Currently learning. Currently building the foundation.
